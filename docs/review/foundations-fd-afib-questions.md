## Atrial fibrillation: anticoagulation and rate control (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS atrial fibrillation guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-af-01** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which items score two points in CHA2DS2-VASc?
  → Prior stroke or TIA + Age 75 or older
- **fd-af-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) At what CHA2DS2-VASc score is anticoagulation recommended for a man with atrial fibrillation?
  → 2 or higher
- **fd-af-03** (single, d2, 4 options) For most patients with atrial fibrillation who need anticoagulation, which is preferred?
  → A direct oral anticoagulant
- **fd-af-04** (multi, d3, 6 options) In which patients is warfarin preferred over a direct oral anticoagulant?
  → A mechanical heart valve + Moderate or severe mitral stenosis
- **fd-af-05** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which criteria reduce the apixaban dose to 2.5 mg twice daily when two of them are present?
  → Age 80 or older + Weight 60 kg or less + Serum creatinine 1.5 mg/dL or higher
- **fd-af-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What is the INR target for warfarin in atrial fibrillation?
  → 2.0 to 3.0
- **fd-af-07** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient has a HAS-BLED score of 3. What does that change?
  → Address the modifiable bleeding risks; anticoagulate anyway
- **fd-af-08** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) For lenient rate control in atrial fibrillation, what resting heart rate is acceptable?
  → Under 110 beats per minute
- **fd-af-09** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient with atrial fibrillation and heart failure with reduced ejection fraction needs rate control. Which class should be avoided?
  → Nondihydropyridine calcium channel blockers
- **fd-af-10** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) Atrial fibrillation of more than 48 hours or unknown duration. How long should anticoagulation precede elective cardioversion, unless a transoesophageal echo excludes thrombus?
  → 3 weeks
- **fd-af-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Which drug reverses dabigatran?
  → Idarucizumab
- **fd-af-12** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient with atrial fibrillation and a high stroke risk cannot take long-term anticoagulation. What is the alternative?
  → Percutaneous left atrial appendage occlusion
