## Acute kidney injury: staging, the first tests, and the drugs to hold (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the KDIGO acute kidney injury guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-aki-01** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) A creatinine rise of at least how much within 48 hours defines acute kidney injury?
  → 0.3 mg/dL
- **fd-aki-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Stage 3 acute kidney injury means creatinine has risen to at least how many times baseline?
  → 3 times
- **fd-aki-03** (single, d2, 4 options) Which cheap test should be done early in every acute kidney injury to exclude obstruction?
  → A bladder scan and kidney ultrasound
- **fd-aki-04** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) A fractional excretion of sodium below what suggests a prerenal cause in a patient not on diuretics?
  → 1%
- **fd-aki-05** (single, d2, 4 options) Which urine finding points to acute tubular necrosis?
  → Muddy brown granular casts
- **fd-aki-06** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which drugs are held during an acute kidney injury?
  → NSAIDs + ACE inhibitors and ARBs + Metformin + SGLT-2 inhibitors
- **fd-aki-07** (multi, d3, 7 options) Which drugs need dose adjustment or extra monitoring in acute kidney injury?
  → Enoxaparin + Gabapentin + Vancomycin + Morphine
- **fd-aki-08** (single, d2, 4 options) What prevents contrast-associated acute kidney injury in a high-risk patient who needs the scan?
  → Intravenous isotonic fluid before and after the contrast
- **fd-aki-09** (single, d2, 4 options) Which antibiotic combination is associated with a higher rate of acute kidney injury than either alone?
  → Vancomycin with piperacillin-tazobactam
- **fd-aki-10** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which are indications for urgent dialysis in acute kidney injury?
  → Refractory hyperkalemia + Refractory acidosis + Volume overload unresponsive to diuretics + Uraemic pericarditis or encephalopathy
- **fd-aki-11** (single, d2, 4 options) What is the role of loop diuretics in acute kidney injury?
  → Treating volume overload only; they do not treat the injury itself
- **fd-aki-12** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) After an episode of acute kidney injury, when should kidney function be rechecked to look for chronic disease?
  → At 3 months
