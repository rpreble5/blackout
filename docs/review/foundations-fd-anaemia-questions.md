## Anaemia: the three patterns and the first tests (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on standard haematology teaching and the BSG and ASH guidance on iron deficiency. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-ane-01** (single, d1, 4 options) Which single test best separates under-production from loss or destruction of red cells?
  → The reticulocyte count
- **fd-ane-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Below what ferritin is iron deficiency confirmed regardless of other tests?
  → 30 ng/mL
- **fd-ane-03** (single, d2, 4 options) A 62-year-old man has iron deficiency anaemia and no obvious source. What does he need?
  → Colonoscopy and upper endoscopy
- **fd-ane-04** (single, d2, 4 options) How is oral iron best dosed for absorption?
  → A single dose every other day
- **fd-ane-05** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) By about how much should haemoglobin rise after 2 to 4 weeks of oral iron if it is working?
  → About 1 g/dL
- **fd-ane-06** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which are causes of a macrocytic anaemia?
  → Vitamin B12 deficiency + Alcohol use + Hypothyroidism + Methotrexate
- **fd-ane-07** (single, d3, 4 options) A B12 level is borderline low in a patient with numbness in the feet. Which test confirms deficiency?
  → Methylmalonic acid
- **fd-ane-08** (single, d2, 4 options) Why is folate not given alone to a patient with an unexplained macrocytic anaemia?
  → It can correct the anaemia while B12 deficiency continues to damage the nervous system
- **fd-ane-09** (multi, d3, 7 options) Which findings point to haemolysis as the cause of an anaemia?
  → A raised reticulocyte count + A raised lactate dehydrogenase + A low haptoglobin + A raised unconjugated bilirubin
- **fd-ane-10** (single, d3, 4 options) A microcytic anaemia with a normal ferritin, a high red cell count and a Mentzer index under 13. What does that suggest?
  → Thalassaemia trait
- **fd-ane-11** (single, d2, 4 options) In anaemia of chronic kidney disease, what is corrected before an erythropoiesis-stimulating agent is considered?
  → Iron deficiency
- **fd-ane-12** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) In a stable hospitalised patient without cardiac disease, below what haemoglobin is transfusion recommended?
  → 7 g/dL
