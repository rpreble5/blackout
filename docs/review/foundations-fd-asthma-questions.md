## Asthma: stepwise therapy and the exacerbation (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the GINA strategy report. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-ast-01** (single, d2, 4 options) Under GINA, what is the preferred reliever for a newly diagnosed adult with mild asthma?
  → As-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol
- **fd-ast-02** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient on as-needed ICS-formoterol has symptoms most days. What is the next step?
  → Low-dose ICS-formoterol as maintenance and reliever
- **fd-ast-03** (multi, d2, 7 options) Over the past four weeks, which findings mean asthma is not well controlled?
  → Daytime symptoms more than twice a week + Any night waking from asthma + Reliever use more than twice a week + Any activity limitation from asthma
- **fd-ast-04** (single, d2, 4 options) Before stepping up asthma treatment, what should be checked first?
  → Inhaler technique, adherence, and modifiable risk factors
- **fd-ast-05** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) In an asthma exacerbation, what oxygen saturation is targeted?
  → 93% to 95%
- **fd-ast-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What course of oral prednisone is standard for an adult asthma exacerbation?
  → 40 to 50 mg daily for 5 to 7 days
- **fd-ast-07** (single, d3, 4 options) A severe exacerbation is not responding to repeated bronchodilators and steroids in the first hour. Which intravenous therapy is added?
  → Magnesium sulfate
- **fd-ast-08** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) A peak flow below what percentage of predicted or personal best marks a severe exacerbation?
  → 50%
- **fd-ast-09** (single, d2, 4 options) On spirometry, what confirms reversible airflow obstruction?
  → FEV1 rises by 12% or more and at least 200 mL after a bronchodilator
- **fd-ast-10** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) After how long of good control should stepping down treatment be considered?
  → About 3 months
- **fd-ast-11** (single, d3, 4 options) Severe asthma remains uncontrolled on high-dose ICS-LABA with good technique and adherence. Which add-on comes before maintenance oral steroids?
  → A biologic chosen by phenotype, such as an anti-IL-5 for eosinophilic disease
- **fd-ast-12** (single, d1, 4 options) What simple instruction reduces oral thrush from inhaled corticosteroids?
  → Rinse the mouth and spit after each dose, and use a spacer with a metered-dose inhaler
