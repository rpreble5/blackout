## Clostridioides difficile: who to test, how to treat, and the recurrence (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2021 IDSA/SHEA Clostridioides difficile guideline update. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-cd-01** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Testing for C. difficile is appropriate after at least how many unformed stools in 24 hours, in a patient not on laxatives?
  → 3
- **fd-cd-02** (single, d2, 4 options) After treatment, should a stool test be repeated to confirm cure?
  → No; the test stays positive for weeks after recovery
- **fd-cd-03** (single, d2, 4 options) What is the preferred treatment for an initial, non-fulminant episode?
  → Fidaxomicin for 10 days
- **fd-cd-04** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which findings define a severe episode?
  → White cell count of 15,000 or more + Creatinine above 1.5 mg/dL
- **fd-cd-05** (single, d3, 4 options) What is the treatment of fulminant C. difficile with ileus?
  → High-dose oral and rectal vancomycin, intravenous metronidazole, and surgical review
- **fd-cd-06** (single, d3, 4 options) A first recurrence after a course of vancomycin. Which regimen is recommended?
  → Fidaxomicin, standard or extended-pulsed, or a tapered and pulsed vancomycin course
- **fd-cd-07** (single, d2, 4 options) After multiple recurrences despite appropriate antibiotics, what is recommended?
  → Faecal microbiota transplantation
- **fd-cd-08** (single, d1, 4 options) Which hand hygiene method removes C. difficile spores?
  → Soap and water
- **fd-cd-09** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which antibiotics carry the highest risk of causing C. difficile infection?
  → Clindamycin + Fluoroquinolones + Third-generation cephalosporins
- **fd-cd-10** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient with C. difficile diarrhoea asks for loperamide. What is the answer?
  → Avoid antimotility drugs until the infection is treated
- **fd-cd-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Which acid-suppressing drug class is associated with C. difficile infection and is reviewed at diagnosis?
  → Proton pump inhibitors
- **fd-cd-12** (single, d2, 4 options) A formed-stool sample from an asymptomatic patient is positive on a screening test. What is done?
  → Nothing; asymptomatic carriage is not treated
