## Cellulitis and abscess: when to drain, when to cover MRSA (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the IDSA skin and soft tissue infection guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-ssti-01** (single, d2, 4 options) Uncomplicated cellulitis with no pus and no systemic signs. Which antibiotic is appropriate?
  → Cephalexin
- **fd-ssti-02** (single, d1, 4 options) What is the primary treatment of a simple skin abscess?
  → Incision and drainage
- **fd-ssti-03** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which oral antibiotics cover community-acquired MRSA?
  → Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole + Doxycycline + Clindamycin
- **fd-ssti-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) How long is a course of antibiotics for uncomplicated cellulitis?
  → 5 days, extended if not improving
- **fd-ssti-05** (single, d3, 4 options) Pain out of proportion to the findings, rapid spread, and crepitus. What is this until proven otherwise, and what is the key step?
  → Necrotising fasciitis; urgent surgical exploration
- **fd-ssti-06** (single, d2, 4 options) Both lower legs are red, warm and swollen in a patient with varicose veins. What is the most likely diagnosis?
  → Stasis dermatitis
- **fd-ssti-07** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient has had four episodes of leg cellulitis in a year. Which preventive measure is recommended?
  → Low-dose penicillin prophylaxis
- **fd-ssti-08** (single, d2, 4 options) Which antibiotic is used after a cat or dog bite?
  → Amoxicillin-clavulanate
- **fd-ssti-09** (single, d3, 4 options) A diabetic foot ulcer has surrounding cellulitis and a probe reaches bone. What does that finding indicate?
  → Probable osteomyelitis
- **fd-ssti-10** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which features move cellulitis from oral to intravenous therapy?
  → Systemic signs such as fever and tachycardia + Failure of oral therapy + Rapid progression + Immunocompromise
- **fd-ssti-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Cellulitis is around a small abscess that has just been drained in a patient with a history of MRSA. Which oral antibiotic fits?
  → Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole
- **fd-ssti-12** (single, d1, 4 options) Besides the antibiotic, what simple measure speeds recovery from leg cellulitis?
  → Elevating the leg
