## Cirrhosis: ascites, infection, encephalopathy, and what to avoid (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on AASLD practice guidance on ascites, hepatic encephalopathy and portal hypertension. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-cir-01** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient with cirrhosis is admitted with ascites. What is done to the ascites on admission?
  → A diagnostic paracentesis
- **fd-cir-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What serum-ascites albumin gradient indicates portal hypertension as the cause of ascites?
  → 1.1 g/dL or higher
- **fd-cir-03** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What ascitic fluid neutrophil count diagnoses spontaneous bacterial peritonitis?
  → 250 cells per microlitre or more
- **fd-cir-04** (single, d3, 4 options) In spontaneous bacterial peritonitis, what does intravenous albumin on days 1 and 3 prevent?
  → Hepatorenal syndrome and death
- **fd-cir-05** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which patients with cirrhosis should be on antibiotic prophylaxis against spontaneous bacterial peritonitis?
  → Anyone who has had an episode + Patients with an acute gastrointestinal bleed + Ascitic protein under 1.5 g/dL with kidney or liver dysfunction
- **fd-cir-06** (single, d2, 4 options) What is the standard diuretic regimen for cirrhotic ascites?
  → Spironolactone and furosemide together in a 100 to 40 ratio
- **fd-cir-07** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) After a large-volume paracentesis of more than 5 litres, how much albumin is given per litre removed?
  → 6 to 8 g
- **fd-cir-08** (single, d2, 4 options) What is first-line treatment for an episode of hepatic encephalopathy?
  → Lactulose titrated to two or three soft stools a day, and a search for the precipitant
- **fd-cir-09** (single, d2, 4 options) Which drug is preferred for primary prevention of variceal bleeding?
  → Carvedilol
- **fd-cir-10** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which drugs should be avoided in a patient with cirrhosis and ascites?
  → NSAIDs + ACE inhibitors and ARBs + Aminoglycosides
- **fd-cir-11** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) How often should patients with cirrhosis have surveillance for hepatocellular carcinoma?
  → Every 6 months
- **fd-cir-12** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) At what MELD score should a patient be referred for transplant evaluation?
  → 15 or higher
