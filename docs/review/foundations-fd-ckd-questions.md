## Chronic kidney disease: staging, slowing it, and when to refer (12 questions)

- **fd-ckd-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) For how long must reduced eGFR or kidney damage persist before chronic kidney disease is diagnosed?
  → 3 months
- **fd-ckd-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Albuminuria category A3 begins at what urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio?
  → 300 mg/g
- **fd-ckd-03** (single, d2, 4 options) Chronic kidney disease with albuminuria. Which drug class is titrated to the highest tolerated dose?
  → An ACE inhibitor or ARB
- **fd-ckd-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Down to what eGFR is an SGLT-2 inhibitor recommended to be started for kidney protection in chronic kidney disease?
  → 20 mL/min per 1.73 m²
- **fd-ckd-05** (multi, d3, 7 options) Which findings are indications for referral to nephrology?
  → eGFR under 30 + Albumin-to-creatinine ratio over 300 mg/g + A fall in eGFR of more than 5 mL/min a year + Persistent unexplained haematuria

  → Finerenone
- **fd-ckd-07** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Below what serum bicarbonate is oral sodium bicarbonate started in chronic kidney disease?
  → 22 mEq/L
- **fd-ckd-08** (single, d2, 4 options) Which imaging contrast is avoided at an eGFR under 30?
  → Older, group I gadolinium agents
- **fd-ckd-09** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) From what age does a patient with chronic kidney disease qualify for a statin regardless of calculated risk?
  → 50
- **fd-ckd-10** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What daily protein intake is advised for adults with chronic kidney disease not on dialysis?
  → 0.8 g per kg
- **fd-ckd-11** (single, d3, 4 options) A creatinine-based eGFR is borderline in a muscular young man. Which test confirms it?
  → Cystatin C, combined with creatinine
- **fd-ckd-12** (single, d2, 4 options) Which vaccine is specifically recommended for adults with chronic kidney disease who may reach dialysis?
