## Delirium: recognising it, finding the cause, and what not to give (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the American Geriatrics Society postoperative delirium guideline and NICE delirium guidance. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-del-01** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which two features must both be present for delirium on the Confusion Assessment Method?
  → Acute onset with a fluctuating course + Inattention
- **fd-del-02** (single, d2, 4 options) Which form of delirium is most common and most often missed?
  → Hypoactive
- **fd-del-03** (single, d2, 4 options) What most reliably distinguishes delirium from dementia at the bedside?
  → Acute onset and fluctuation, with impaired attention
- **fd-del-04** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which are common, correctable causes of delirium in a hospitalised older patient?
  → Infection + Urinary retention + Constipation + Uncontrolled pain
- **fd-del-05** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which drug classes most often precipitate delirium?
  → Anticholinergics + Benzodiazepines + Opioids
- **fd-del-06** (single, d2, 4 options) What is the first-line management of delirium once the cause is being treated?
  → Non-drug measures: reorientation, sleep, glasses and hearing aids, mobility, family presence
- **fd-del-07** (single, d3, 4 options) When is an antipsychotic justified in delirium?
  → Severe agitation that endangers the patient or others, at the lowest dose for the shortest time
- **fd-del-08** (single, d2, 4 options) In which single situation are benzodiazepines the right treatment for delirium?
  → Alcohol or benzodiazepine withdrawal
- **fd-del-09** (single, d2, 4 options) Which sleep aid should not be given to an older hospitalised patient?
  → Diphenhydramine
- **fd-del-10** (single, d2, 4 options) When is neuroimaging indicated in a patient with delirium?
  → Focal neurological signs, head trauma, anticoagulation, or no cause found on initial work-up
- **fd-del-11** (single, d2, 4 options) A family asks how long the confusion will last. What is an honest answer?
  → Days to weeks, and sometimes longer; some patients never fully return to baseline
- **fd-del-12** (single, d2, 4 options) Which of these increases delirium rather than preventing it?
  → Physical restraints
