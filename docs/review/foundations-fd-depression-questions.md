## Depression: screening, first-line drugs, and what to do when they fail (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on USPSTF screening guidance and the APA major depressive disorder guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-dep-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) From what PHQ-9 score is depression considered at least moderate?
  → 10
- **fd-dep-02** (single, d2, 4 options) Before starting an antidepressant, which history must be asked about specifically?
  → Past episodes of mania or hypomania
- **fd-dep-03** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which are reasonable first-line antidepressants for a first episode of moderate depression?
  → Sertraline + Escitalopram + Bupropion + Mirtazapine
- **fd-dep-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) How long is an adequate trial of an antidepressant at a therapeutic dose before judging it a failure?
  → 6 to 8 weeks
- **fd-dep-05** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) After remission of a first episode, how long should the antidepressant be continued?
  → 4 to 9 months
- **fd-dep-06** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient has a partial response after eight weeks of sertraline at a good dose. Which strategy fits?
  → Augment, for example with bupropion or an atypical antipsychotic
- **fd-dep-07** (single, d2, 4 options) Which antidepressant should be avoided in a patient with a seizure disorder or an eating disorder?
  → Bupropion
- **fd-dep-08** (single, d2, 4 options) An older patient on an SSRI develops confusion. Which laboratory abnormality is the SSRI most likely to have caused?
  → Hyponatremia
- **fd-dep-09** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which drugs raise the risk of serotonin syndrome when combined with an SSRI?
  → Tramadol + Linezolid + An MAOI
- **fd-dep-10** (single, d2, 4 options) Which antidepressants cause the worst discontinuation symptoms when stopped abruptly?
  → Paroxetine and venlafaxine
- **fd-dep-11** (single, d2, 4 options) A 19-year-old is starting an SSRI. What does the boxed warning about suicidality change in practice?
  → Closer follow-up in the first weeks, not withholding treatment
- **fd-dep-12** (single, d3, 4 options) Which antidepressant is usually preferred for a breastfeeding mother with postpartum depression?
  → Sertraline
