## Type 2 diabetes: diagnosis, targets, and choosing therapy (15 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the ADA Standards of Care and standard primary care practice. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-dm-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) At what haemoglobin A1c is diabetes diagnosed?
  → 6.5% or higher
- **fd-dm-02** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) At what fasting plasma glucose is diabetes diagnosed?
  → 126 mg/dL or higher
- **fd-dm-03** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) What is the A1c target for most nonpregnant adults with diabetes?
  → Under 7%
- **fd-dm-04** (single, d1, 4 options) Which drug is first-line for most adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes and no heart or kidney disease?
  → Metformin
- **fd-dm-05** (single, d2, 4 options) Type 2 diabetes with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction. Which class is recommended regardless of A1c?
  → An SGLT-2 inhibitor
- **fd-dm-06** (multi, d2, 5 options) Type 2 diabetes with established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. Which classes have proven cardiovascular benefit?
  → A GLP-1 receptor agonist + An SGLT-2 inhibitor
- **fd-dm-07** (single, d2, 4 options) Type 2 diabetes with chronic kidney disease and albuminuria. Which class slows kidney decline?
  → An SGLT-2 inhibitor
- **fd-dm-08** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Below what eGFR should metformin be stopped?
  → 30 mL/min per 1.73 m²
- **fd-dm-09** (single, d2, 4 options) Which deficiency should be checked periodically in patients on long-term metformin?
  → Vitamin B12
- **fd-dm-10** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which classes carry a meaningful risk of hypoglycaemia on their own?
  → Sulfonylureas + Insulin
- **fd-dm-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Which history is a contraindication to a GLP-1 receptor agonist?
  → Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2
- **fd-dm-12** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) For how long before major surgery should an SGLT-2 inhibitor be held?
  → At least 3 days
- **fd-dm-13** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) At presentation, insulin is recommended from the start when A1c exceeds what?
  → 10%
- **fd-dm-14** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What is the usual starting dose of basal insulin in type 2 diabetes?
  → 0.1 to 0.2 units per kg per day
- **fd-dm-15** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) How often should urine albumin and eGFR be checked in type 2 diabetes without known kidney disease?
  → At least annually
