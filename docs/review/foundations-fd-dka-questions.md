## Diabetic ketoacidosis and hyperosmolar state: the first hours (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the ADA consensus on hyperglycaemic crises. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-dka-01** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which findings define diabetic ketoacidosis?
  → Ketones in blood or urine + Arterial pH under 7.30 + Bicarbonate under 18 mEq/L
- **fd-dka-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Above what serum osmolality is the hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic state diagnosed?
  → 320 mOsm/kg
- **fd-dka-03** (single, d1, 4 options) What is the first treatment in diabetic ketoacidosis, before insulin?
  → Intravenous isotonic fluid, about a litre in the first hour
- **fd-dka-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Below what potassium is insulin withheld until potassium has been replaced?
  → 3.3 mEq/L
- **fd-dka-05** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What is the standard insulin infusion rate in diabetic ketoacidosis?
  → 0.1 units per kg per hour
- **fd-dka-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) In diabetic ketoacidosis, at what glucose is dextrose added to the fluids?
  → 200 mg/dL
- **fd-dka-07** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which findings mark resolution of diabetic ketoacidosis?
  → Bicarbonate 15 mEq/L or higher + pH above 7.30 + Anion gap 12 or under
- **fd-dka-08** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) How long before stopping the insulin infusion should the first subcutaneous basal insulin be given?
  → 1 to 2 hours
- **fd-dka-09** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) Below what pH is sodium bicarbonate considered in diabetic ketoacidosis?
  → 6.9
- **fd-dka-10** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which are common precipitants of diabetic ketoacidosis?
  → Infection + Missed insulin + Myocardial infarction + SGLT-2 inhibitors
- **fd-dka-11** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient on empagliflozin has abdominal pain, vomiting, ketones and a pH of 7.2 with a glucose of 180 mg/dL. What is this?
  → Euglycaemic diabetic ketoacidosis
- **fd-dka-12** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) How often are electrolytes checked during treatment of diabetic ketoacidosis?
  → Every 2 to 4 hours
