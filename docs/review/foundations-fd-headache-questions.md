## Headache: the red flags, and treating the common ones (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the SNNOOP10 red-flag framework, the International Classification of Headache Disorders, and the American Headache Society treatment guidance. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-ha-01** (multi, d2, 8 options) Which headache features are red flags that need urgent investigation?
  → Sudden onset reaching maximum within a minute + New headache after age 50 + A change in the usual pattern + Headache brought on by exertion or Valsalva + Papilloedema
- **fd-ha-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) A thunderclap headache. Within how many hours of onset does a normal non-contrast CT effectively exclude subarachnoid haemorrhage?
  → 6 hours
- **fd-ha-03** (single, d2, 4 options) A 72-year-old has a new temporal headache, jaw pain on chewing, and a raised ESR. What is done first?
  → Start high-dose corticosteroids now, before the biopsy
- **fd-ha-04** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) An obese young woman with headache and papilloedema has a normal MRI. Above what lumbar puncture opening pressure is idiopathic intracranial hypertension diagnosed?
  → 25 cm of water
- **fd-ha-05** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which features support a diagnosis of migraine without aura?
  → Attacks lasting 4 to 72 hours + Unilateral, pulsating pain + Worse with routine activity + Nausea or sensitivity to light and sound
- **fd-ha-06** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient has typical migraine with a normal neurological examination. Is neuroimaging needed?
  → No
- **fd-ha-07** (multi, d2, 7 options) In which patients are triptans contraindicated?
  → Coronary artery disease + Uncontrolled hypertension + Hemiplegic migraine + Prior stroke
- **fd-ha-08** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) Using simple analgesics on how many days a month, for more than three months, defines medication overuse headache?
  → 15 or more
- **fd-ha-09** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) From how many migraine days a month is preventive treatment generally offered?
  → 4 or more
- **fd-ha-10** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which are first-line oral preventives for migraine?
  → Topiramate + Propranolol + Amitriptyline + Candesartan
- **fd-ha-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Severe unilateral orbital pain lasting an hour with tearing and a runny nose on the same side, recurring daily for weeks. What is the acute treatment?
  → High-flow oxygen or subcutaneous sumatriptan
- **fd-ha-12** (single, d2, 4 options) Which acute migraine drug is preferred in pregnancy?
  → Acetaminophen, with metoclopramide for nausea
