## Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction: diagnosis and what actually helps (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA heart failure guideline and the 2023 focused update. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-hfp-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction is defined by an ejection fraction of at least what?
  → 50%
- **fd-hfp-02** (single, d2, 4 options) Which drug class has the strongest evidence for reducing hospitalisation in HFpEF?
  → SGLT-2 inhibitors
- **fd-hfp-03** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which drugs carry a weaker, class 2b, recommendation in HFpEF, particularly at the lower end of the preserved range?
  → Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists + Sacubitril-valsartan + ARBs
- **fd-hfp-04** (single, d2, 4 options) Which HFrEF drug has no proven benefit in HFpEF when used only for the heart failure?
  → Beta blockers
- **fd-hfp-05** (single, d2, 4 options) What are loop diuretics for in HFpEF?
  → Relieving congestion, at the lowest dose that keeps the patient euvolemic
- **fd-hfp-06** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which comorbidities drive HFpEF and are treated as part of its management?
  → Hypertension + Obesity + Atrial fibrillation + Sleep apnoea
- **fd-hfp-07** (single, d3, 4 options) In patients with HFpEF and obesity, which treatment improved symptoms and exercise capacity in a randomised trial?
  → Semaglutide
- **fd-hfp-08** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which echo findings support HFpEF in a patient with dyspnoea and a normal ejection fraction?
  → Left atrial enlargement + Left ventricular hypertrophy + A raised E/e' ratio
- **fd-hfp-09** (single, d3, 4 options) An older man has HFpEF, thick ventricular walls on echo, and low voltage on his ECG. What should be considered?
  → Cardiac amyloidosis
- **fd-hfp-10** (single, d2, 4 options) Which long-acting drug was tested in HFpEF and failed to improve activity levels?
  → Isosorbide mononitrate
- **fd-hfp-11** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient with HFpEF has a heart rate of 130 from atrial fibrillation and worsening dyspnoea. Why does the arrhythmia matter so much here?
  → The stiff ventricle depends on atrial contraction and a long filling time
- **fd-hfp-12** (single, d2, 4 options) Which blood test raises the probability of heart failure in a breathless patient with a normal ejection fraction?
  → A raised natriuretic peptide
