## Hypertension: diagnosing, choosing drugs, and targets (15 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2017 ACC/AHA hypertension guideline and standard primary care practice. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-htn-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) Under the ACC/AHA definition, at what office blood pressure does stage 1 hypertension begin?
  → 130/80 mm Hg
- **fd-htn-02** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which classes are first-line for uncomplicated hypertension?
  → A thiazide-type diuretic + An ACE inhibitor + An ARB + A dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker
- **fd-htn-03** (single, d2, 4 options) A Black adult with hypertension and no heart failure or kidney disease. Which initial therapy is preferred?
  → A thiazide-type diuretic or a calcium channel blocker
- **fd-htn-04** (single, d2, 4 options) Hypertension with chronic kidney disease and albuminuria. Which class should be part of the regimen?
  → An ACE inhibitor or ARB
- **fd-htn-05** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) What is the blood pressure target for most adults being treated for hypertension?
  → Under 130/80 mm Hg
- **fd-htn-06** (single, d2, 4 options) When is starting two antihypertensives at once recommended rather than one?
  → Stage 2 hypertension, with blood pressure 20/10 mm Hg or more above target
- **fd-htn-07** (single, d2, 4 options) Which combination should be avoided?
  → An ACE inhibitor with an ARB
- **fd-htn-08** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) After starting an ACE inhibitor, up to what rise in creatinine is acceptable without stopping the drug?
  → About 30%
- **fd-htn-09** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient on losartan becomes pregnant. What should happen to the losartan?
  → Stop it now and switch to a pregnancy-safe agent
- **fd-htn-10** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which antihypertensives are preferred in pregnancy?
  → Labetalol + Nifedipine + Methyldopa
- **fd-htn-12** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient started on amlodipine three weeks ago has new bilateral ankle swelling with no other findings. What is the most likely cause?
  → The amlodipine
- **fd-htn-13** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Resistant hypertension means blood pressure above goal despite how many drugs at optimal doses, one of them a diuretic?
  → 3
- **fd-htn-14** (single, d3, 4 options) Which drug is the usual fourth agent in resistant hypertension?
  → Spironolactone
- **fd-htn-15** (single, d2, 4 options) Office readings are consistently high but the patient says home readings are normal. What confirms or refutes hypertension?
  → Out-of-office measurement, by home or ambulatory monitoring
- **fd-htn-16** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) After starting or changing an antihypertensive, when should blood pressure be reassessed?
  → In about a month
