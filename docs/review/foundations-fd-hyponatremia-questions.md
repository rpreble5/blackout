## Hyponatremia: working it up and correcting it safely (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the European and US expert panel hyponatremia guidelines. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-hna-01** (single, d1, 4 options) What is the first test that sorts out a low sodium?
  → Serum osmolality
- **fd-hna-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) By how much is the measured sodium corrected upward for each 100 mg/dL of glucose above normal?
  → About 1.6 to 2.4 mEq/L
- **fd-hna-03** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which findings support SIADH in a euvolemic patient with hypotonic hyponatremia?
  → Urine osmolality above 100 mOsm/kg + Urine sodium above 30 mEq/L + Normal thyroid and adrenal function
- **fd-hna-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What is the maximum safe rise in sodium over 24 hours in chronic hyponatremia?
  → 8 mEq/L
- **fd-hna-05** (multi, d3, 7 options) Which factors raise the risk of osmotic demyelination during correction?
  → Sodium under 105 mEq/L + Alcohol use disorder + Malnutrition + Hypokalemia
- **fd-hna-06** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient with a sodium of 116 is having a seizure. What is the treatment?
  → A 100 to 150 mL bolus of 3% saline over 10 to 20 minutes, repeated up to three times
- **fd-hna-07** (single, d2, 4 options) Hypovolemic hyponatremia from vomiting and diarrhoea. What is the treatment?
  → Isotonic saline
- **fd-hna-08** (single, d2, 4 options) Which is the first-line treatment for hyponatremia caused by SIADH?
  → Fluid restriction
- **fd-hna-09** (single, d3, 4 options) Sodium has risen 10 mEq/L in 12 hours after treatment. What is done?
  → Give desmopressin and free water to bring the sodium back down
- **fd-hna-10** (single, d2, 4 options) Which class of diuretic most often causes hyponatremia?
  → Thiazides
- **fd-hna-11** (multi, d2, 5 options) Which endocrine conditions must be excluded before diagnosing SIADH?
  → Hypothyroidism + Adrenal insufficiency
- **fd-hna-12** (single, d3, 4 options) A heavy beer drinker who eats little has a sodium of 118 with a urine osmolality of 60 mOsm/kg. What is the mechanism?
  → Too little solute intake to excrete the water load
