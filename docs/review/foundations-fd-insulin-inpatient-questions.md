## Insulin in hospital: targets, regimens, hypoglycaemia, discharge (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the ADA Standards of Care, diabetes care in the hospital. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-ins-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) What is the glucose target range for most hospitalised patients?
  → 140 to 180 mg/dL
- **fd-ins-02** (single, d2, 4 options) Why is sliding-scale insulin alone discouraged for hospitalised patients with diabetes?
  → It treats hyperglycaemia after the fact and never prevents it
- **fd-ins-03** (single, d2, 4 options) A non-critically ill patient with type 2 diabetes is eating normally. Which insulin regimen is preferred?
  → Basal plus prandial plus correction
- **fd-ins-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What total daily insulin dose is a usual starting point for an insulin-naive hospitalised patient?
  → 0.3 to 0.5 units per kg
- **fd-ins-05** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient on basal-bolus insulin is made nil by mouth for a procedure. What happens to the insulin?
  → Continue basal, usually at a reduced dose, and hold the prandial doses
- **fd-ins-06** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) Below what glucose is hypoglycaemia defined in hospital?
  → 70 mg/dL
- **fd-ins-07** (single, d2, 4 options) A conscious patient who can swallow has a glucose of 62 mg/dL. What is the first treatment?
  → 15 grams of fast carbohydrate, then recheck in 15 minutes
- **fd-ins-08** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient started on prednisone 40 mg each morning has glucose readings that climb through the afternoon and settle overnight. Which insulin adjustment fits that pattern?
  → A morning dose of NPH insulin
- **fd-ins-09** (single, d2, 4 options) Which oral class is generally not recommended for use during a hospital admission?
  → SGLT-2 inhibitors
- **fd-ins-10** (single, d2, 4 options) When should metformin be held in hospital?
  → Around iodinated contrast, and with acute kidney injury, sepsis or hypoxia
- **fd-ins-11** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient admitted on metformin alone has an admission A1c of 11%. What should the discharge regimen include?
  → Basal insulin added to the metformin
- **fd-ins-12** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) The evening before surgery, what fraction of a patient's usual long-acting basal insulin should be given?
  → About 75% to 80%
