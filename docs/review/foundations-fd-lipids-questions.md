## Lipids: who gets a statin, how much, and what next (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2018 ACC/AHA cholesterol guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-lip-01** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which patients belong to a statin benefit group regardless of their calculated ten-year risk?
  → Clinical atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease + LDL cholesterol of 190 mg/dL or higher + Diabetes at age 40 to 75
- **fd-lip-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) In primary prevention, above what ten-year ASCVD risk is a statin recommended?
  → 7.5%
- **fd-lip-03** (multi, d2, 5 options) Which regimens count as high-intensity statin therapy?
  → Atorvastatin 40 to 80 mg + Rosuvastatin 20 to 40 mg
- **fd-lip-04** (single, d2, 4 options) A 55-year-old has an LDL of 205 mg/dL and no other risk factors. What is the recommendation?
  → A high-intensity statin now, without a risk calculation
- **fd-lip-05** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) In very high-risk ASCVD on a maximal statin, above what LDL is adding ezetimibe recommended?
  → 70 mg/dL
- **fd-lip-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) After starting or changing a statin, when should lipids be rechecked?
  → In 4 to 12 weeks
- **fd-lip-07** (single, d3, 4 options) An intermediate-risk patient is unsure about a statin. A coronary artery calcium score comes back at zero. What does that support?
  → Withholding the statin for now and reassessing in 5 to 10 years
- **fd-lip-08** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient on atorvastatin reports aching legs with a normal creatine kinase. What is the usual next step?
  → Stop it, wait for symptoms to resolve, then rechallenge or try another statin at a lower dose
- **fd-lip-09** (multi, d3, 7 options) Which are risk enhancers that favour a statin when ten-year risk is borderline?
  → Family history of premature ASCVD + Chronic kidney disease + Metabolic syndrome + South Asian ancestry
- **fd-lip-10** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Above what triglyceride level does pancreatitis become the main concern?
  → 500 mg/dL
- **fd-lip-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Which statin dose is no longer recommended because of myopathy risk?
  → Simvastatin 80 mg
- **fd-lip-12** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient with diabetes aged 40 to 75 and no other risk factors. What intensity of statin is recommended?
  → At least moderate intensity
