## Osteoporosis: who to screen, who to treat, and with what (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on USPSTF screening recommendations and the Bone Health and Osteoporosis Foundation clinician's guide. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-ost-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) From what age should all women be screened for osteoporosis with a DXA scan?
  → 65
- **fd-ost-02** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) At or below what T-score is osteoporosis diagnosed?
  → -2.5
- **fd-ost-03** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which patients should be offered treatment?
  → A T-score of -2.5 or lower + A hip or vertebral fragility fracture + Osteopenia with a ten-year hip fracture risk of 3% or more
- **fd-ost-04** (single, d1, 4 options) What is first-line drug treatment for most patients with osteoporosis?
  → An oral bisphosphonate such as alendronate
- **fd-ost-05** (single, d2, 4 options) How should alendronate be taken?
  → On an empty stomach with a full glass of water, staying upright for 30 minutes
- **fd-ost-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Below what creatinine clearance is alendronate contraindicated?
  → 35 mL/min
- **fd-ost-07** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) After how many years of an oral bisphosphonate can a drug holiday be considered in a patient at moderate risk?
  → 5 years
- **fd-ost-08** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient on denosumab wants to stop it. What must happen?
  → Transition to a bisphosphonate; do not simply stop
- **fd-ost-09** (single, d3, 4 options) Which patient is a candidate for an anabolic agent such as teriparatide or romosozumab first?
  → Very high fracture risk: a T-score of -3.0 or lower, or multiple or recent fractures
- **fd-ost-10** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What total daily calcium intake, from diet and supplements together, is recommended for postmenopausal women?
  → 1200 mg
- **fd-ost-11** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which secondary causes should be looked for in a new diagnosis of osteoporosis?
  → Vitamin D deficiency + Primary hyperparathyroidism + Coeliac disease + Long-term glucocorticoids
- **fd-ost-12** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) Long-term prednisone at what daily dose, for three months or more, triggers a bone protection assessment?
  → 7.5 mg or more
