## Perioperative medicine: which drugs to hold, and who needs testing (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the ACC/AHA perioperative cardiovascular evaluation guideline and standard perioperative medicine practice. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-peri-01** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient on long-term metoprolol is having elective surgery. What happens to the beta blocker?
  → Continue it, including the morning of surgery
- **fd-peri-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) How many days before elective surgery is warfarin stopped?
  → 5 days
- **fd-peri-03** (multi, d3, 6 options) Which patients on warfarin need bridging with heparin around surgery?
  → A mechanical mitral valve + Venous thromboembolism within the past 3 months + Atrial fibrillation with a recent stroke
- **fd-peri-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) For a procedure with high bleeding risk, how long before surgery is apixaban stopped in a patient with normal kidney function?
  → 2 days
- **fd-peri-05** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) After a drug-eluting coronary stent, how long should elective surgery that requires stopping the P2Y12 inhibitor be delayed?
  → At least 6 months
- **fd-peri-06** (single, d2, 4 options) Which drug should be held on the morning of surgery?
  → Metformin
- **fd-peri-07** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) Above what daily prednisone dose, taken for more than three weeks, is adrenal suppression assumed and stress-dose steroid considered?
  → 20 mg
- **fd-peri-08** (multi, d3, 8 options) Which are components of the Revised Cardiac Risk Index?
  → High-risk surgery + Ischaemic heart disease + Heart failure + Insulin-treated diabetes + Creatinine over 2 mg/dL
- **fd-peri-09** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) A patient at elevated cardiac risk can climb two flights of stairs without symptoms. What functional capacity does that represent, and what does it mean for testing?
  → 4 METs or more; proceed to surgery without stress testing
- **fd-peri-10** (single, d2, 4 options) Which preoperative tests are routine for a healthy patient having a low-risk operation?
  → None
- **fd-peri-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Which screening tool identifies patients at risk of obstructive sleep apnoea before surgery?
  → STOP-BANG
- **fd-peri-12** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) How far before surgery does stopping smoking measurably reduce complications?
  → At least 4 weeks
