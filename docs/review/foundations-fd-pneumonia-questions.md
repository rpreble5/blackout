## Community-acquired pneumonia: severity, antibiotics, and follow-up (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2019 ATS/IDSA community-acquired pneumonia guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-cap-01** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which items make up the CURB-65 score?
  → Confusion + Urea above 7 mmol/L + Respiratory rate 30 or more + Blood pressure under 90 systolic or 60 diastolic + Age 65 or older
- **fd-cap-02** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which are appropriate for outpatient pneumonia in a healthy adult with no recent antibiotics?
  → Amoxicillin 1 g three times daily + Doxycycline
- **fd-cap-03** (single, d2, 4 options) Outpatient pneumonia in a patient with COPD and diabetes. Which regimen fits?
  → Amoxicillin-clavulanate plus a macrolide or doxycycline, or a respiratory fluoroquinolone alone
- **fd-cap-04** (single, d2, 4 options) What is the standard regimen for a patient admitted with non-severe pneumonia?
  → A beta-lactam such as ceftriaxone plus a macrolide
- **fd-cap-05** (multi, d3, 6 options) When is empirical MRSA or Pseudomonas cover added in community-acquired pneumonia?
  → Prior isolation of the organism from the respiratory tract + Hospitalisation with intravenous antibiotics in the past 90 days, with locally validated risk
- **fd-cap-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What is the minimum duration of antibiotics for community-acquired pneumonia?
  → 5 days, and clinically stable
- **fd-cap-07** (single, d2, 4 options) Should blood and sputum cultures be sent for an outpatient with pneumonia?
  → No
- **fd-cap-08** (single, d2, 4 options) Can a low procalcitonin be used to withhold antibiotics in a patient with a clinical and radiographic diagnosis of pneumonia?
  → No; antibiotics are started regardless
- **fd-cap-09** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient with pneumonia tests positive for influenza. What is added?
  → Oseltamivir, regardless of how long symptoms have lasted, if admitted
- **fd-cap-10** (single, d2, 4 options) Suspected aspiration pneumonia in a patient with dysphagia. Is anaerobic coverage added?
  → Not routinely; only with a lung abscess or empyema
- **fd-cap-11** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient has recovered from pneumonia within a week. Is a follow-up chest radiograph needed?
  → No, not routinely
- **fd-cap-12** (single, d3, 4 options) Are corticosteroids recommended for non-severe community-acquired pneumonia?
  → No
