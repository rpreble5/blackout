## Stroke and TIA: the urgent work-up and secondary prevention (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2021 AHA/ASA secondary stroke prevention guideline and the 2019 acute ischaemic stroke guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-str-01** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient describes an hour of arm weakness and slurred speech yesterday, now fully resolved. What is the urgency?
  → Evaluation within 24 to 48 hours, with imaging, vascular imaging and an ECG
- **fd-str-02** (single, d2, 4 options) Which imaging best confirms that a transient event was ischaemic and shows any infarct?
  → MRI with diffusion-weighted imaging
- **fd-str-03** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) After a minor stroke or high-risk TIA, dual antiplatelet therapy with aspirin and clopidogrel is given for how long?
  → 21 days
- **fd-str-04** (single, d2, 4 options) A stroke is attributed to atrial fibrillation. What is the long-term prevention?
  → Oral anticoagulation, usually a direct oral anticoagulant
- **fd-str-05** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Symptomatic carotid stenosis of 70% to 99%. Within how long should endarterectomy be done for greatest benefit?
  → 2 weeks
- **fd-str-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) After an atherosclerotic ischaemic stroke, what LDL cholesterol is targeted?
  → Under 70 mg/dL
- **fd-str-07** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What blood pressure target is recommended after a stroke or TIA?
  → Under 130/80 mm Hg
- **fd-str-08** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Up to how many hours from symptom onset can intravenous thrombolysis be given for ischaemic stroke?
  → 4.5 hours
- **fd-str-09** (single, d3, 4 options) A 45-year-old has a cryptogenic stroke and a patent foramen ovale with a large shunt. Which intervention reduces recurrence?
  → Percutaneous closure of the foramen
- **fd-str-10** (single, d2, 4 options) An ischaemic stroke with no atrial fibrillation found on the admission ECG or telemetry. What is done about the rhythm?
  → Prolonged monitoring, since paroxysmal atrial fibrillation is often missed
- **fd-str-11** (single, d2, 4 options) After a stroke, which is recommended for secondary prevention in a smoker with diabetes?
  → Smoking cessation, glucose control, a statin, blood pressure control and an antithrombotic
- **fd-str-12** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient on aspirin has a second ischaemic stroke. What is the long-term antiplatelet plan?
  → A single agent, often switched to clopidogrel; not permanent dual therapy
