## Syncope: red flags, the first tests, and who goes home (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2017 ACC/AHA/HRS syncope guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-syn-01** (multi, d1, 6 options) Which are part of the initial evaluation of every patient with syncope?
  → A detailed history including witnesses + Physical examination with orthostatic vital signs + A 12-lead ECG
- **fd-syn-02** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which features point to a cardiac cause of syncope?
  → Syncope during exertion + Syncope while lying down + No warning at all + A family history of sudden death before 50
- **fd-syn-03** (single, d1, 4 options) What is the most common cause of syncope in a young, otherwise healthy patient?
  → Vasovagal syncope
- **fd-syn-04** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Orthostatic hypotension is a fall in systolic pressure of at least how much within three minutes of standing?
  → 20 mm Hg
- **fd-syn-05** (single, d2, 4 options) When is an echocardiogram indicated after syncope?
  → When structural heart disease is suspected from the history, examination or ECG
- **fd-syn-06** (single, d2, 4 options) A patient faints, recovers within a minute, and has a normal neurological examination. Is a head CT indicated?
  → No; neuroimaging is not part of the routine work-up of syncope
- **fd-syn-07** (single, d2, 4 options) Which feature distinguishes a seizure from syncope most reliably?
  → A prolonged period of confusion after the event
- **fd-syn-08** (single, d3, 4 options) Recurrent unexplained syncope with a normal initial work-up and a low suspicion of arrhythmia. Which test helps confirm reflex syncope?
  → Tilt-table testing
- **fd-syn-09** (multi, d3, 7 options) Which ECG findings in a patient with syncope warrant specialist referral?
  → A prolonged QT interval + A Brugada pattern + Bifascicular block + Ventricular pre-excitation
- **fd-syn-10** (single, d2, 4 options) A 70-year-old faints when turning his head or shaving. Which bedside test is appropriate?
  → Carotid sinus massage with ECG and blood pressure monitoring
- **fd-syn-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Syncope while coughing, urinating or swallowing. What is this?
  → Situational reflex syncope
- **fd-syn-12** (single, d2, 4 options) Which patient with syncope should be admitted?
  → One with a suspected cardiac cause
