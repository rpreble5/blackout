## Hypothyroidism and the abnormal TSH (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the American Thyroid Association hypothyroidism guidelines. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-thy-01** (single, d1, 4 options) Which pattern defines primary hypothyroidism?
  → High TSH with low free T4
- **fd-thy-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) What is the usual full replacement dose of levothyroxine?
  → About 1.6 micrograms per kg per day
- **fd-thy-03** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) After a levothyroxine dose change, when should TSH be rechecked?
  → In 6 to 8 weeks
- **fd-thy-04** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which should be separated from levothyroxine by about four hours?
  → Calcium supplements + Iron supplements + Proton pump inhibitors
- **fd-thy-05** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) In subclinical hypothyroidism, at what TSH is treatment generally recommended?
  → 10 mIU/L or higher
- **fd-thy-06** (single, d3, 4 options) A woman on levothyroxine confirms a pregnancy. What is the immediate dose change?
  → Increase the dose by about 25% to 30% now
- **fd-thy-07** (single, d3, 4 options) Low free T4 with a low or inappropriately normal TSH. What does this suggest, and what is followed during treatment?
  → Central hypothyroidism; follow free T4
- **fd-thy-08** (single, d2, 4 options) A hospitalised patient with pneumonia has a mildly low TSH and low T3. What is the right response?
  → Recheck 6 weeks after recovery; do not treat now
- **fd-thy-09** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which drugs commonly cause hypothyroidism?
  → Amiodarone + Lithium + Immune checkpoint inhibitors
- **fd-thy-10** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient taking high-dose biotin has a low TSH and a high free T4 but no symptoms. What is the most likely explanation?
  → Assay interference from the biotin
- **fd-thy-11** (single, d2, 4 options) Which antibody confirms Hashimoto thyroiditis as the cause of hypothyroidism?
  → Anti-thyroid peroxidase
- **fd-thy-12** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which harms follow long-term over-replacement with a suppressed TSH?
  → Atrial fibrillation + Bone loss
