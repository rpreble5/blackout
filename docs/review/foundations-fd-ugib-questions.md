## Upper gastrointestinal bleeding: triage, resuscitation, endoscopy (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the 2021 ACG upper gastrointestinal and ulcer bleeding guideline and AASLD variceal guidance. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-ugib-01** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) At or below what Glasgow-Blatchford score can a patient with upper GI bleeding be managed as an outpatient?
  → 1
- **fd-ugib-02** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Below what haemoglobin is transfusion recommended in upper GI bleeding without cardiovascular disease?
  → 7 g/dL
- **fd-ugib-03** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Within how many hours of presentation should endoscopy be performed for non-variceal upper GI bleeding?
  → 24 hours
- **fd-ugib-04** (single, d2, 4 options) What is the role of an intravenous proton pump inhibitor before endoscopy?
  → Reasonable to give; it reduces high-risk stigmata but does not change mortality
- **fd-ugib-05** (multi, d3, 6 options) A patient with known cirrhosis presents with haematemesis. Which treatments are started before endoscopy?
  → Octreotide infusion + Prophylactic ceftriaxone + Resuscitation to a haemoglobin of 7 to 8
- **fd-ugib-06** (single, d3, 4 options) Which drug given 30 to 90 minutes before endoscopy improves the view by emptying the stomach?
  → Intravenous erythromycin
- **fd-ugib-07** (single, d2, 4 options) A bleeding ulcer is found. Which infection is tested for and treated?
  → Helicobacter pylori
- **fd-ugib-08** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient on aspirin for a prior myocardial infarction has a bleeding ulcer treated endoscopically. When does the aspirin restart?
  → As soon as haemostasis is secure, usually within days
- **fd-ugib-09** (single, d2, 4 options) Which laboratory finding points to an upper rather than lower source in a patient with red blood per rectum?
  → A raised urea out of proportion to creatinine
- **fd-ugib-10** (single, d3, 4 options) Endoscopy shows an ulcer with a clean base. What endoscopic treatment is needed?
  → None
- **fd-ugib-11** (single, d3, 4 options) An ulcer rebleeds after endoscopic therapy. What is the next step?
  → A second endoscopic attempt
- **fd-ugib-12** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) After a bleeding duodenal ulcer, how long is oral proton pump inhibitor therapy continued for healing?
  → 4 to 8 weeks
