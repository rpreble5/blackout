## Urinary tract infection: cystitis, pyelonephritis, and when not to treat (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on IDSA guidance on cystitis, pyelonephritis and asymptomatic bacteriuria. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-uti-01** (multi, d2, 6 options) Which are first-line regimens for uncomplicated cystitis in a woman?
  → Nitrofurantoin for 5 days + Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole for 3 days + Fosfomycin as a single dose
- **fd-uti-02** (single, d2, 4 options) Why is nitrofurantoin not used for pyelonephritis?
  → It does not reach adequate levels in kidney tissue
- **fd-uti-03** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) For outpatient pyelonephritis treated with ciprofloxacin, how long is the course?
  → 7 days
- **fd-uti-04** (multi, d2, 7 options) In which situations should a urine culture be sent before treating?
  → Pyelonephritis + Pregnancy + Recurrent infection + A man with urinary symptoms
- **fd-uti-05** (multi, d2, 6 options) Asymptomatic bacteriuria should be treated in which patients?
  → Pregnant women + Patients about to have a urological procedure with mucosal bleeding
- **fd-uti-06** (single, d3, 4 options) A patient with a catheter in place for three weeks has fever and a positive urine culture. What should happen before treating?
  → Replace the catheter and send the culture from the new one
- **fd-uti-07** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) For how long is cystitis in a man usually treated?
  → 7 days
- **fd-uti-08** (multi, d3, 5 options) Which antibiotics are avoided for cystitis in pregnancy?
  → Fluoroquinolones + Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole in the first trimester
- **fd-uti-09** (single, d2, 4 options) A postmenopausal woman has three culture-proven cystitis episodes in a year. Which preventive measure has the best evidence?
  → Vaginal oestrogen
- **fd-uti-10** (single, d2, 4 options) On a urine dipstick, which finding is the more specific for infection?
  → Nitrite
- **fd-uti-11** (single, d2, 4 options) A woman treated for cystitis is well after her course. Should a test-of-cure urine be sent?
  → No; resolution of symptoms is the end point
- **fd-uti-12** (single, d3, 4 options) Pyelonephritis is not improving after 48 to 72 hours of appropriate antibiotics. What is the next step?
  → Imaging for obstruction or abscess
