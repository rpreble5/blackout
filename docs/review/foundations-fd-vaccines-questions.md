## Vaccines for adults: who gets what (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the ACIP adult immunisation schedule. Ages and products change yearly; review before trusting.

- **fd-vac-01** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) From what age is a pneumococcal conjugate vaccine recommended for all adults?
  → 50
- **fd-vac-02** (single, d2, 4 options) A 55-year-old has never had a pneumococcal vaccine. Which single option completes the series in one dose?
  → PCV20 or PCV21
- **fd-vac-03** (single, d1, 4 options, fixed order) The recombinant zoster vaccine is recommended in two doses from what age?
  → 50
- **fd-vac-04** (single, d2, 4 options) Which influenza vaccines are preferred for adults 65 and older?
  → A high-dose, adjuvanted, or recombinant vaccine
- **fd-vac-05** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) How often is a tetanus-containing booster given after the adult Tdap?
  → Every 10 years
- **fd-vac-06** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) During which weeks of pregnancy is Tdap given?
  → 27 to 36 weeks
- **fd-vac-07** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Up to what age is HPV vaccination routinely recommended for adults who were not vaccinated earlier?
  → 26
- **fd-vac-08** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) Hepatitis B vaccination is now recommended universally for adults up to what age?
  → 59
- **fd-vac-09** (single, d2, 4 options) Who is recommended to receive a single dose of RSV vaccine?
  → All adults 75 and older, and adults 60 to 74 at increased risk
- **fd-vac-10** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which vaccines are live and therefore avoided in pregnancy and severe immunocompromise?
  → MMR + Varicella + Live attenuated influenza
- **fd-vac-11** (multi, d2, 7 options) Which adults need meningococcal vaccination?
  → Asplenia + Complement deficiency or complement inhibitor therapy + First-year college students living in dormitories who missed it + Travel to the meningitis belt or the Hajj
- **fd-vac-12** (single, d2, 4 options) A healthcare worker born in 1985 has no record of MMR and no serology. What is recommended?
  → Two doses of MMR at least 28 days apart
