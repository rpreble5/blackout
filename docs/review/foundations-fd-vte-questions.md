## Venous thromboembolism: choosing the anticoagulant and how long (12 questions)

Source: Written for Blackout, 2026-09-13. Based on the CHEST 2021 antithrombotic therapy for VTE guideline. Not tied to an article; review before trusting.

- **fd-vte-01** (single, d2, 4 options, fixed order) A deep vein thrombosis after hip surgery. How long is anticoagulation?
  → 3 months
- **fd-vte-02** (single, d2, 4 options) A first unprovoked pulmonary embolism in a patient with low bleeding risk. How long is anticoagulation?
  → Extended, with no scheduled stop date, reassessed each year
- **fd-vte-03** (single, d2, 4 options) For most patients with venous thromboembolism and no cancer, which anticoagulant is preferred?
  → A direct oral anticoagulant
- **fd-vte-04** (single, d3, 4 options) Venous thromboembolism in a patient with active cancer. Which is preferred?
  → A direct oral anticoagulant or low molecular weight heparin, while the cancer is active
- **fd-vte-05** (single, d3, 4 options) After 6 months of full-dose treatment, a patient continues extended anticoagulation. What dose is reasonable?
  → Reduced dose: apixaban 2.5 mg twice daily or rivaroxaban 10 mg daily
- **fd-vte-06** (single, d3, 4 options) Venous thromboembolism in a patient with antiphospholipid syndrome. Which anticoagulant?
  → Warfarin
- **fd-vte-07** (single, d2, 4 options) An isolated distal calf vein thrombosis in a patient with mild symptoms and no risk factors for extension. Which option is reasonable?
  → Serial ultrasound over two weeks rather than immediate anticoagulation
- **fd-vte-08** (single, d2, 4 options) A second unprovoked venous thromboembolism. How long is anticoagulation?
  → Indefinitely
- **fd-vte-09** (single, d2, 4 options) A haemodynamically stable patient with a low-risk pulmonary embolism, a simplified PESI of zero, and good support at home. Where is treatment given?
  → At home, or after a short observation period
- **fd-vte-10** (single, d2, 4 options) When is an inferior vena cava filter indicated?
  → Acute venous thromboembolism when anticoagulation is contraindicated
- **fd-vte-11** (single, d3, 4 options, fixed order) Superficial vein thrombosis 8 cm long, near the saphenofemoral junction. How long is prophylactic-dose fondaparinux given?
  → 45 days
- **fd-vte-12** (single, d2, 4 options) Which test can wait until well after acute treatment of a deep vein thrombosis has started?
  → Thrombophilia testing
